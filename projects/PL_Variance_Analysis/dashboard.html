Key Insights
  • Revenue exceeded budget by $83.9M (+23.3%) — primarily driven by Net Interest Income outperformance as interest rate environment favoured NovaStar's asset mix.
  • OpEx ran $13.8M over budget (+8.0%) — Personnel costs were the largest driver due to mid-year headcount expansion and retention bonuses. Technology investments also exceeded plan.
  • EBITDA Margin of 53.4% — 6.5 percentage points above the 46.9% budget target, demonstrating strong operating leverage.
  • Net Income of $207.7M — a record for NovaStar, representing 19.7% YoY growth and 45.0% above the budget plan.
